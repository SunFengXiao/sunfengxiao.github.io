Biography

Fengxiao Sun is a Researcher and Tenured Associate Professor at the School of Physics, Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications, and a supervisor for doctoral and master's students. He received his B.S. in Physics from Peking University in 2015 and his Ph.D. from the Institute of Modern Optics, Peking University, in 2020.

His research focuses on theoretical studies of quantum optics and atomic physics, as well as their applications in quantum information. He has published more than 40 SCI-indexed papers in leading journals including Physical Review Letters, Light: Science & Applications, Science Bulletin, and Laser & Photonics Reviews.

He leads projects funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China, including General Program and Theoretical Physics Special Program projects, and serves as a reviewer for journals such as Physical Review Letters, Physical Review X, and npj Quantum Information.

Research Interests

Quantum Optics and Quantum Information

Detection of quantum entanglement in multimode non-Gaussian systems; preparation and control of quantum resources such as Schrödinger cat states; and applications in quantum communication, quantum computing, and quantum metrology.

Learn More →

Quantum Phase Transitions and Topological Properties

Investigation and control of quantum phase transitions in light–atom coupled systems and topological phase transitions in nonlocal coupled systems.

Learn More →

Quantum Properties of Ultrafast Optical Fields

Characterization and study of quantum features in strong-field phenomena, including above-threshold ionization and high-harmonic generation driven by quantum optical fields such as bright squeezed light.
